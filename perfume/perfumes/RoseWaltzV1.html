Rose Waltz V1

Sultry Rose And Spiced Ibrik

Rose Waltz is an idea: what if rose was dark, alluring, and seductive. Something the draws you in, something that isn't the typical bright floral.

The basic idea most perfumers go for is a "noir" style - with gothic notes and animalic facets.

However, this rose doesn't focus on removing the sweet jammy essence of rose, in fact, it colludes with it with chai-like spices and a moreish dark coffee.

Opening with a Yemeni coffee tincture that's at the base of the dilutant, it comforts you with gentle intoxication.

Lulls you into a false safety, opening sweet and fruity with an in-house grass and cherry accord, only to start drawing you into darker depths with lactonic chai duo of cardamom and cloves.

At the heart of this composition is the complex intertwining of delicate white rose and a blend of jammy Bulgarian and Turkish red roses that's perfumed with coffee

As the sweetness dies out the spicy resins and smokey woods drown out your senses all the while giving you a sweet pillow of vanilla and orris to drift away on.

Rose Waltz is the alluring night time captured into a bottle, a night of opportunity and mistakes.

Perfume Architecture

Top Mandarin, Grass and Cherry accord, Bitter Orange, Buchu
Top-Heart Cardamom, Coconut, Petitgrain, Frankincense
Heart Coffee, Clove Bud, Geranium, White Rose, Rose Bulgaria X Turkey
Base-Heart Vanilla, Tobacco, Patchouli, Tonka, Vetiver, Orris
Base Trat, Cedarwood, Labdanum, Benzoin, Firewood
