Pefume idea: Rule of Thirds

Here is a small perfume structure that I've been planning based on doubles and halves:
The idea is you have 2/3 of one material and then use 1/3 to transmute it to be more than the sum of their product.
For example, for 3g of an attar we'll use 2g of rose and 1g of oud - a simple 2:1 ratio.
Double the rose, add have of the rose total as oud to help shift the rose profile.

I've expanded this thought to encompass the entire perfume pyramid for a simple composition.

TOP NOTES - 15%
MIDDLE NOTES - 50%
BASE NOTES - 35%
